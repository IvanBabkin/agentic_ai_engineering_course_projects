While it is acknowledged that stubbornness can complicate training, this personality trait also signifies a dachshund's intelligence and strong will, allowing for a deep connection with their owner as they learn together. Moreover, successful training techniques can harness their independence, with many owners attesting that the challenge leads to a rewarding bond that enhances their affection.

As for health issues, while dachshunds are prone to certain conditions, responsible breeding, regular exercise, and preventive veterinary care can significantly mitigate these risks. Furthermore, their loyalty and devotion create a companionship that often outweighs the potential challenges, bringing immeasurable joy and love into countless homes—qualities that make them truly the best dog breed.
