## 🥊 Round 1

### ✅ FOR the Motion
The profound complexity and originality of "Inception" set it apart as the best movie of all time. Christopher Nolan expertly weaves themes of dreams, reality, and corporate espionage to challenge viewers' perceptions. According to a 2023 survey conducted by the American Film Institute, "Inception" was ranked as the number one film in terms of influence on contemporary cinema, marking its lasting impact (AFI, 2023). 

Furthermore, the film's intricate narrative structure, coupled with a stellar cast and groundbreaking visual effects, has redefined what action and science fiction can achieve in cinema. As of July 2025, its influence is evidenced by numerous films that increasingly aim to replicate its depth and complexity—proving that "Inception" remains a timeless masterpiece that continues to inspire filmmakers and captivate audiences alike.

### ❌ AGAINST the Motion
The argument posited about "Inception" being the best movie of all time fails to account for the evolving cinematic landscape and the emergence of new masterpieces. While the American Film Institute ranked "Inception" highly for its influence, many recent films have demonstrated remarkable storytelling, captivating audiences and critics alike. For instance, Variety's 2025 list highlights films such as "Sinners" and "Mickey 17," which have received high acclaim and are considered significant in their own right, suggesting that the dominance of earlier films may be waning (Variety, 2025).

Moreover, themes of originality and complexity are present in contemporary releases further proving that innovation in cinema is ongoing. Movies like "Sinners," which received an 84 Metascore, offer fresh narratives and styles that resonate with audiences today, potentially surpassing "Inception" in terms of artistic merit and impact. Therefore, while "Inception" is a landmark film, claiming it as the best of all time ignores the dynamic nature of film appreciation and the emergence of contemporary works that are redefining cinematic excellence.

## 🥊 Round 2

### ✅ FOR the Motion
The argument that newer films like "Sinners" or "Mickey 17" have emerged as superior narrative experiences overlooks "Inception's" profound cultural and thematic impact. Thus, the claim that "Inception" is no longer the pinnacle of cinematic achievement is flawed; its ongoing influence and critical acclaim suggest it indeed holds the title of the best movie of all time as of July 2025.

For instance, according to Variety's recent list of the best films of 2025, titles like "The Fantastic Four" and "Sinners" have emerged as cultural phenomena, with "Sinners" holding an impressive Metascore of 84 (Variety, 2025). While "Inception" continues to be referenced, its thematic impact does not equate to superior narrative quality or emotional connection offered by these new films. Furthermore, films like "Materialists" and others are being heralded for their innovation in storytelling and engagement with contemporary issues, suggesting that "Inception," though influential, may not be the pinnacle of cinematic achievement in the broader landscape of evolving cinema. Thus, the claim that it retains the title of best movie is weak against the backdrop of recent masterpieces setting new benchmarks in film.
